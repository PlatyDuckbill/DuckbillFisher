FFX|V Fish Tracker App / Carbuncle Plushy
https://github.com/icykoneko/ff14-fish-tracker-app
Revision: d0e1ab5bba62c2b57356e690a73676cd4751b64d

Derived material: 903 rod-fishing and 203 spearfishing records from private/fishData.yaml
and js/app/data.js; weather target calculation adapted from js/app/weather.js.
Changes: selected KR-matched IDs and conditions with the development importer,
preserved missing-data markers and alternative baits, excluded four records
without a matching KR log row, omitted bite-time statistics;
weather target arithmetic expressed in C#. Displayed game names, icons, maps, and weather
rates are read from the installed client and are not copied from this project.
The upstream weather implementation credits Rougeadyn's SaintCoinach library.
