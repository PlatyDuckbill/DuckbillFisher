# 0.5.0.0

## New feature: Spawn schedule

Plan your next fishing target from the new Spawn schedule tab.

- Switch between a compact timeline and a monthly calendar. Toggle a single target button between favorites and the main list's filtered fish; refine the result with search and hide-caught.
- Start with a 3-hour timeline, or choose 1, 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours. Compact fish-icon rows show fishing spots and regions. Hover over an interval for exact times and shared-time highlights; click it for fish details.
- Preview spawn time and fish name on the monthly calendar. Click a date for all its intervals, drag the divider to resize the list, or hide the list for a larger calendar. Only the selected month is calculated.
- Open map pins directly and toggle the shared Fish Eyes forecast assumption from its toolbar icon.

Forecasts use bundled Korean-server conditions and local PC times. Always-available fish, unknown conditions and unsupported schedules are not presented as invented timed intervals. Movement, preparation, actual buffs and catch success are not included.
