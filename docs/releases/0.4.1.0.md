# 0.4.1.0

- Newly discovered fishing spots now update the verified catch record and trip location.
- Fishing history has resizable columns, total fishing time in lists and details, fish/bait icons, clearer outcomes, and separate result timing.
- Confirmed mooch casts show a connection to the preceding successful catch.
- Recent timer contents stay visible for five minutes after stopping, even across area changes. A new trip replaces them.

## Other improvements

- Refined collection-progress colors into a consistent blue, teal, and green palette.
