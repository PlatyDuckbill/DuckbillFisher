# 0.6.0.0 — New feature · Fishing recommendations

Choose your next big-fish target in the new Recommendations tab, with appearance schedules, mooch chains and Intuition requirements together.

- Fish to target now: see candidates available now or within 30 minutes.
- Prep work: browse candidates requiring mooch bait or Intuition catches, with prerequisite appearance schedules.
- Least frequent: browse fish by fewest appearances over 30 days and check their next appearance.
- Narrow candidates with the time range and filters, then refresh for the latest appearance schedules.

Other improvements:

- Local chat alerts notify you when uncaught big or legendary fish appear at your current fishing hole.
- Spawn alerts now support notifications at the start of an appearance or up to 120 minutes beforehand.
- Spawn alerts show the time remaining and link directly to fish details.
- A Fish Guide shortcut appears across all main tabs when collection status needs refreshing.
