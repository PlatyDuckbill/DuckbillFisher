# 0.4.0.0

## New feature: Collection progress

Track your fishing log progress, from the overall picture to each region, in the new Collection progress page.

- Check a large regular-fishing summary and ocean-fishing progress by sea area.
- Compare expansions and regions using all fish, big fish including legendary fish, or legendary fish views.
- Progress bars adapt to the view width. Hover for full counts, percentages, and unknown states.
- Click an expansion, region, or sea-area bar to open a fish list. The list stays open until you close it, and clicking another area reuses it.
- Switch between all fish and hiding caught fish. Manual collection settings take priority, and unknown fish remain visible.

## Other improvements

- The compact-list button in the main view now toggles it open and closed.
- Added icons to the main navigation tabs.
