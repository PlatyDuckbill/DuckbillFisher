# 0.5.3.0 — Fishing sound alerts

Choose sounds for bites and first big or legendary catches.

- Choose individual built-in sound effects or custom WAV files for bites, big fish, and legendary fish.
- Trophy fish sounds play only on first fish guide registration by default. Disable first acquisition only to play on every confirmed catch.

Other improvements:

- Keep the fishing-spot list's collection filter after restarting the plugin or game.
- Use separate cast alert and fishing sound tabs in timer settings, with icons, bullet help, and common output settings at the upper right.

Notes:

Custom sounds use local WAV files up to 10 MB / 30 seconds and follow Windows volume. Choose, preview, reload or stop each sound individually. Cast-time alert presets retain their existing scope.

Settings use schema 21. Existing favorites, manual collection choices, filters, cast alerts, presets and fishing history are preserved. No fishing actions are automated and no runtime network synchronization is added. Verified runtime support remains limited to the supported Korean client and Dalamud module combinations.
