# 0.8.0.0 — Intuition preparation and attempts

Track Intuition preparation and keep a record of your big-fish attempts.

- Track Intuition preparation in the casting timer, including a new count after Intuition ends.
- Automatically record one attempt per appearance of an uncaught big fish while fishing at its hole.

Other improvements:

- Find carried baits unrelated to the current fish filters and their Intuition prerequisites.
- Open fish details by name with /dbfs or from fish-item context menus.
- View Double Hook and Triple Hook yield guidance for regular fish where data is available.
- See actual multiple-catch quantities in fishing history.
