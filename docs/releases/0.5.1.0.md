# 0.5.1.0

- The casting timer shows Patience and Fisher's Intuition icons with their live remaining time. Inactive effects and unavailable observations are distinguished.
- Hide currently available fish whose remaining availability is shorter than your minimum time left.
- Set independent minimum and maximum times until spawn. A minimum alone keeps fish starting at least that many minutes from now; a maximum alone keeps fish starting within that many minutes. Both bounds include the entered values.
- Clear any time-filter field to remove that limit. Minimum time left also accepts 0 to disable it. Settings and presets preserve the limits; the compact list and current-list bait preparation/schedule share them. The compact list's own upcoming horizon still applies.

Times are real minutes. Unknown times and always-available fish are retained by these filters. An inverted upcoming range shows a message and cannot match known upcoming times. Buff observation is separate from spawn forecasts and fishing history. No fishing actions are automated.
