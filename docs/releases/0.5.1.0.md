# 0.5.1.0 — Fishing timers and time filters

Follow fishing buff timers and narrow fish lists by remaining time.

- The casting timer shows icons and live remaining time for Patience and Fisher's Intuition.
- Set a minimum time left to hide currently available fish that end too soon.
- Set independent minimum and maximum times until spawn. Use either bound alone or both together.
- Clear any time-filter field to remove that limit. Settings and presets preserve the limits; the compact list and current-list preparation/schedule share them.

Notes:

Times are real minutes. Unknown times and always-available fish are retained by these filters. An inverted upcoming range shows a message and cannot match known upcoming times. Buff observation is separate from spawn forecasts and fishing history. No fishing actions are automated.
